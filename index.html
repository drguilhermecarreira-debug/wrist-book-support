Privacy

Wrist Book keeps your collection on your iPhone. Photos, references, amounts paid, and the values you save stay on the device. The app has no account and does not send that information to a server.

The camera and photo library are used only when you add a picture of a watch or its papers. Opening Chrono24 leaves the app and opens the site in your browser.

If you write for support, that message goes to the email address above.
